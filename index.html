Valencia Office Events 2026

Cinco de Mayo Tacos

Join us 11am–1pm for fresh made tacos, salsa, guac, churros & more catered by Angel City Taco Catering. 100% organic & made fresh. ¡Feliz Cinco de Mayo!

Office Craft Day

Join your fellow Scorpions for libations and finding your inner artist! We'll be doing a sip and paint style craft session where everyone creates something they can proudly display on their desk or mantle at home. No artistic talent required.

June

Food Tour of the World

Help us kick off the FIFA World Cup by going on a culinary world tour! We're ordering in dishes from countries competing in the tournament so come hungry, vote for your favorites, and may the best dish win.

World Cup Trivia

Compete in our very own World Cup of Trivia! We'll have rounds covering soccer, world culture, pop culture, and more so whether you're a die hard football fan or couldn't name a single player, there's something for everyone. Prizes up for grabs so bring your A game.

July 4th BBQ

Good things come to those who wait and this BBQ spread is no exception. We're celebrating America's 250th and the best season of the year with slow cooked food and even better company.

Think you're the master of spicy foods? Well then don't sweat this challenge. Work your way up from mild to scorching and feel the Scorpion sting. Prizes for the brave, bragging rights for the rest.

Board & Brush

Time to get crafty! Board & Brush is bringing their instructor-led DIY wood sign workshop straight to the office. Choose your design, distress, stain, and paint your very own one-of-a-kind piece to take home. No artistic ability needed — just good vibes and a willingness to get your hands dirty.

Fall Bake-Off

Need to practice that new pie recipe before Thanksgiving? Try it on your fellow Scorpions first! Bring your best dessert, compete for prizes, and let us be your taste testers. Pies, cheesecakes, whatever satisfies that sweet tooth. You batter believe its going to be good.

October

MLB Playoff Bracket

Play ball! The MLB Playoffs are here and it's time to fill out your bracket and root for your team. Join us for our bracket kickoff event and compete for prizes as we follow the road to the World Series throughout October. Step up to the plate and may the best pick win.

Halloween + Trick or Treat

It's the most spook-tacular time of the year! Bring the little ghouls and goblins to the office for trick or treating, enjoy some scary good treats, and show off your costume. We dare you to out-dress your coworkers. No tricks, just treats!

Friendsgiving + Cocktail Class

Friendsgiving is here and we are so grateful for this team! Join us for a holiday lunch with your fellow Scorpions and then stick around to learn some festive holiday cocktails and mocktails from a local mixologist. The only thing better than the food is the company.

Puppy Day + Toy Drive

Tis the season of giving and we are making it a little more fun! Drop off a new unwrapped toy for a local charity and get some quality time with some adorable therapy dogs. It's a win win. Do it for the kids, stay for the puppy kisses.

Cookie Swap

Santa likes cookies and so do Scorpions! Bring your best batch to compete for prizes in our annual cookie swap. Sweet, savory, classic, or creative, all cookies welcome. May the best cookie win!
